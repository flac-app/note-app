**Minimalist sticky notes app for those who like to take notes often and need them easily accessible.**

## Why was note. made?

The main reason behind the creation of note. was because I needed a convenient way of quickly taking notes without opening a whole notepad or Word doc while playing TTRPGs online. I desperately needed something that can stay in my view without taking up the whole screen and not require me to alt-tab, because it is a pain _(and quite distracting)_ to Alt+Tab constantly especially if you have multiple things open to begin with.

Second reason was because I feel unproductive and often forget things if I don't take notes, note. helps with that.

## Features

- **Notes library**: Create, search and open all of your notes from one place, as a list or a grid of note cards.
- **Edge tabs**: Tuck your notes off-screen to get rid of on screen clutter, the notes will be displayed with a small icon on the screen edge. Click to pull out. Customizable.
- **Rich text**: Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bullets, text alignment and hyperlinks.
- **Images**: Drag and drop, paste or insert images, resize them.
- **Note links**: Link notes to each other, or to files and folders for ease of access.
- **Checklists**: Checklist system so you can track your work easier.
- **Reminders**: Notes have the ability to set up timed reminders, with custom sounds and pop-up notifs.
- **App triggers**: You can choose certain notes to appear automatically whenever you open another app.
- **Shortcuts**: `Ctrl+Alt+N` makes a new note from anywhere. Every shortcut can be changed.
- **Your look**: Any custom color, adjustable transparency, notebook-style worn paper and tabs, and dark mode.
- **Private**: The notes ared saved automatically to your PC. No account required and no cloud involvement.

## Install

Download from **[Releases](https://github.com/flac-app/note-app/releases/latest)**:

- **Installer** (`note.app-<version>-setup.exe`): no admin rights needed. Your notes are not removed if you uninstall.
- **Portable** (`note.app.exe`): one file, just click and run.

To launch **note.** when Windows starts, turn on **⚙ → Open on system startup** in the library.
